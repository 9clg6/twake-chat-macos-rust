## Acceptance criteria (from e2e test bodies)

### e2eC: Calls

- [calls] Given a call is started in a room When it is live Then `call-stage` shows, `call-window` is absent; "Detach" puts the call in `call-window` while the conversation (`composer`) takes the room back; the window menu offers corners and a full size; in another room the window stays with `leave-call`; back in its room it is still detached; expanding returns to `call-stage`, no window; leaving removes `call-stage`.
- [calls] Given a call is live on Chrome with the tab-switch action When the tab is left Then the call pops out in its own window (Document PiP) with its controls, drawn once (neither `call-stage` nor `call-window` in the page), the composer stays; closing the PiP returns it to `call-stage` with `leave-call`, no `call-window`; hanging up from the PiP closes it and ends the call. Without PiP support nothing is offered and the call stays in the room (D618).
- [calls] Given a call in its window When the menu item `call-window-pop-out` is clicked Then the call goes out of the tab (a click is the gesture); on Safari the menu has `call-window-expand` and no `call-window-pop-out`; closing the PiP returns to `call-window`.
- [calls] Given a call starts When it shows Then the room list folds (`room-list` hidden); user can bring it back; after the call ends the list is visible; a new call in the same room, or in another room opened from the list, folds it again (D591).
- [calls] Given "Do not disturb" on (toggle `toggle-do_not_disturb` checked, Personalisation, this device) When someone calls Then no `incoming-call` dialog, but the room shows `ongoing-call`.
- [calls] Given bob starts a call in a group When alice is anywhere in the app Then `incoming-call` dialog (modal, middle of screen, D134) contains "is calling you in Call <room name>" and "bob" (case-insensitive), with `answer-call` visible; at a 700 px viewport its width is under 500 px.
- [calls] Given a ring (D108) When the page is allowed to play Then it rings as a phone every 2 s (notes played); declined, it stays quiet while the call lasts, reload included (no new notes after 3 s). The ring lasts 2 min per a test comment (60 s per AGENTS.md).
- [calls] Given the ring was declined When the call goes on Then `start-call` has aria-label matching `Join the call (1 ` (count of people), and `ongoing-call` (green banner under the header) contains "A call is going on, 1 person"; after joining via `start-call` then `join-call`, the banner is gone and `call-tile` count is 2; the declined call never rings again.
- [calls] Given two people in a call When shown Then `call-stage` is wider than twice `call-conversation`; `composer` visible; the room list folds; tiles take the largest size at which all fit (no scroll, wider than the old 280 px row); only the own camera is flipped, the others never (D309); bob's video decodes and sound flows; bob sees 2 tiles.
- [calls] Given screen share via `call-share` When active Then `call-tile` count is 3, the stage tile (`data-stage`) takes the full grid width (>= grid width - 1) and the people tiles are in a row under it (top >= stage bottom); toggling again returns to 2.
- [calls] Given bob speaks in the call When it redraws Then the call re-renders but the room list does not (search field `sidebar-search` is not rewritten; changes.call > 0, changes.list = 0).
- [calls] Given the microphone control When the call shows Then it is `aria-pressed="true"` (asked once, on at once), `aria-label="Microphone"` (name constant, state in aria-pressed), toggles false/true; its arrow lists 2 inputs with the first `aria-checked="true"`; picking the other makes it checked within 15 s, call continues, no `call-error`.
- [calls] Given the camera is refused by the browser (NotAllowedError) When `call-camera` is clicked Then `call-error` contains "could not be opened" and `call-camera` stays `aria-pressed="false"`.
- [calls] Given the user leaves while bob stays When the call ends for her Then `call-stage` is gone, the room list is back, `ongoing-call` is visible (offers to rejoin), and no `incoming-call` rings; the picked microphone device is used for the next call, even after reload (D570, D573).
- [calls] Given a call in a channel (clear) When Meet is available Then `call-stage` has `data-encrypted="false"`; `call-hand` click makes it `aria-pressed="true"` and bob sees the hand on her tile (named by id until profile loads), cleared on lowering; `call-subtitles` becomes `aria-pressed="true"` with no `call-error`.
- [calls] Given `call-record` is clicked (Meet v1.25 may refuse with 403) Then either `call-recording` badge appears (and bob sees recording) or `call-error` shows with `call-record` `aria-pressed="false"` and no recording; never a badge for a recording that is not happening (D572). When recording runs, `call-transcription` is `aria-pressed="true"` and `call-record` is `aria-disabled="true"` (one recording per room); clicking transcription ends the badge. When refused, transcription is `aria-pressed="false"`.
- [calls] Given a call in an encrypted room (D430) Then `call-stage` has `data-encrypted="true"`, no `call-record`, no `call-transcription`, but `call-hand` and `call-microphone` are visible; bob's frames decode (key shared by MatrixRTC) and sound flows.
- [calls] Given the Video tile in room info of a channel Then `join-call` (screen before the call) is visible; the channel header has exactly one `start-call`, left of `header-search`, and `start-call` and search have equal width and height (>0) (D315).
- [calls] Given one person on two devices Then the button counts people while the grid counts devices (3 tiles on both devices with bob), and after 5 s nobody is thrown out.
- [calls] Given alice leaves while bob stays Then after 6 s no `incoming-call` rings her; bob still has tiles.
- [calls] Given alice starts a call in a channel (D105) Then the column beside the call is the call thread: toggle `call-thread-toggle` has text "Show the channel", column contains "Meeting thread"; a message sent there shows in a list named "Replies of the thread. - [calls] Given Chat framed in Twake Space with `?call=start` (D119, D47) Then the frame shows `join-call` but no `start-call`, no `header-favourite`, `info-toggle` visible; when the call runs the frame has `data-fullscreen="true"` and the call-thread toggle does not overlap the floating side-pane button; after the call `data-fullscreen="false"`.
